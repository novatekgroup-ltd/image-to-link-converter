# 🌙 About NovaTek Image to Link Converter

**A minimalist, privacy‑friendly tool that turns any image into a permanent, shareable link – entirely in your browser, with zero API keys.**

## Why we built this

Many image hosting services require registration, API keys, or impose short‑lived links. We wanted a **no‑hassle, dark‑by‑default** solution for our own workflow, and decided to share it with the community.

## Key principles

- **Zero configuration** – open the page, drag an image, get a link.
- **No server‑side tracking** – your image is sent directly from your browser to the hosting service (0x0.st). We never see or store your files.
- **Permanent links** – as long as 0x0.st maintains the file, your link stays valid.
- **Dark mode first** – easier on the eyes during late‑night work.
